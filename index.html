Biography

About Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim

Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim (1987–2024) was a distinguished researcher in biophysics and biochemistry who left a lasting impact on the international scientific community.

He graduated in 2008 from the Department of Biophysics, Faculty of Science, Ain Shams University, with Excellent with Honors — ranking first in his class.

In 2011 he was awarded the Erasmus Mundus Scholarship to pursue his Master's in Protein Chemistry at Lund University, Sweden, completing his thesis in 2012 at the Technical University of Berlin. He earned his PhD at Humboldt University of Berlin (2012–2016), researching the function of photosynthetic proteins — particularly Photosystem II (PSII) — using X-ray Free Electron Lasers (XFEL).

He continued as a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Holger Dobbek's group until January 2022, then joined Prof. Rolf Hilgenfeld's group at the University of Lübeck. His later work expanded into drug design, targeting coronavirus proteases.

Throughout his career he published more than 30 articles in high-impact international journals, received the Young Investigator Award in 2020, and contributed to a patent. His scientific legacy continues to inspire young researchers through this initiative.

Flagship Program

Mohamed Ibrahim Academic Excellence Scholarship

Supporting outstanding Egyptian and refugee Arab students and graduates who demonstrate exceptional academic performance and a strong commitment to postgraduate studies in STEM.

Scholarship Coverage

Annual financial support that may be used to cover:

  • Standardized examination fees (TOEFL, IELTS, GRE, etc.)
  • University application fees for postgraduate studies
  • Academic and research-related opportunities

$1,000 total annual award, distributed between selected recipients.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must:

  • Be final-year undergraduate students or recent graduates
  • Be pursuing studies in STEM fields (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic excellence
  • Show strong commitment to research and postgraduate education
  • Demonstrate financial need

Selection Process

Applications are reviewed independently in collaboration with Egypt Scholars Inc. based on:

  • Academic merit
  • Research potential
  • Future academic goals
  • Financial need

The family of Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim does not interfere in the selection process — ensuring fairness and transparency.

Administration

The initiative is supported by the family of Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim and administered in collaboration with Egypt Scholars Inc.

Future scholarship programs, research travel opportunities, and academic initiatives may be added in the coming years.
