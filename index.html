Biography

About Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim

Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim (1987–2024) was a biophysicist and structural biologist whose scientific career spanned photosynthesis research and structure-based drug discovery. After graduating with first-class honors from Ain Shams University, he was awarded an Erasmus Mundus Scholarship and continued his scientific training in Sweden and Germany. His research focused on understanding Photosystem II using advanced structural methods, including X-ray free-electron lasers (XFEL), before later expanding into the structural study of coronavirus proteases and antiviral drug design. Throughout his career, Mohamed contributed to several scientific publications in highly impactful journals. His dedication to science and research continues to inspire the mission of the Mohamed Ibrahim Initiative.

Our mission

Empowering the Next Generation of Scientists

We believe that a person's impact does not end with their passing. The knowledge they leave behind, the good they set in motion, and the difference they make in the lives of others can continue for generations.

It is from this belief that the Mohamed Ibrahim Initiative was born—in memory of Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim (1987–2024) and as a continuation of his scientific and human legacy. Mohamed devoted his life to science and research, believed deeply in the value of perseverance and learning, and left an impact through his research, his colleagues, and the young scientists around him.

The Initiative began with Mohamed's legacy, but its vision reaches further: to turn the impact of science into a lasting legacy and to create opportunities for young scientists and researchers to build their own paths, so that they, in turn, can leave an impact that extends beyond them to future generations.

Through scholarships, awards, and academic and scientific support programs, the Initiative seeks to invest in people and in science. We believe that supporting a young researcher today may be the beginning of beneficial knowledge and an impact that continues for generations.

From one legacy to another... we believe that a single opportunity can be the beginning of a scientific journey, and that every young scientist we support today may create a new legacy tomorrow.

May Allah accept this work as Sadaqah Jariyah for Mohamed's soul and grant him the reward of every beneficial knowledge advanced, every opportunity opened, and every positive impact that continues through this Initiative.

Flagship Program

Mohamed Ibrahim Academic Excellence Scholarship

The Mohamed Ibrahim Academic Excellence Scholarship is one of the scholarship programs established under the Mohamed Ibrahim Scholarship Initiative. The scholarship supports outstanding Egyptian and refugee Arab students and graduates who demonstrate exceptional academic performance and a strong commitment to pursuing postgraduate studies in STEM fields.

Scholarship Coverage

The scholarship provides annual financial support that may be used to cover:

  • Standardized examination fees (TOEFL, IELTS, GRE, etc.)
  • University application fees for postgraduate studies
  • Academic and research-related opportunities

Currently, the scholarship provides a total annual award of $1,000, distributed between selected recipients.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must:

  • Be final-year undergraduate students or recent graduates
  • Be pursuing studies in STEM fields (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics)
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic excellence
  • Show strong commitment to research and postgraduate education

Selection Process

Applications are reviewed independently in collaboration with Egypt Scholars Inc. based on:

  • Academic merit
  • Research potential
  • Future academic goals

The family of Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim does not interfere in the selection process to ensure fairness and transparency.

Administration

The Mohamed Ibrahim Scholarship Initiative (Mohamed Ibrahim Academic Excellence Scholarship) is supported by the family of Dr. Mohamed Ibrahim and administered in collaboration with Egypt Scholars Inc.

Future scholarship programs, research travel opportunities, and academic initiatives may be added under the initiative in the coming years.
